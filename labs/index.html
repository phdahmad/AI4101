    Rules

    • Work in pairs, and write one report per pair.
    • Name the PDF AI4101_LabN_<ID1>_<ID2>.pdf: the lab number, then your two student IDs.
    • Submit the report the way your instructor announces in class.
    • You may use an AI assistant to help with your English. The results, the screenshots and the ideas must be your own, and the last section of every report says which tools you used and for what.
